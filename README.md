# YouTube Lite

A browser extension that gives users complete control over their YouTube homepage and recommendations by filtering unwanted content and prioritizing videos that match their interests.

## Features

### 🎯 Keyword-Based Feed Filtering
- Show videos containing specific keywords (e.g., UI/UX, Programming, AI, Finance).
- Hide videos containing unwanted keywords (e.g., Pranks, Vlogs, Gaming).
- Supports multiple include and exclude keywords.
- Instantly updates the YouTube feed based on your preferences.

### 🚫 Hide YouTube Shorts
- Completely remove the Shorts section from:
  - Home page
  - Search results
  - Subscription feed
  - Sidebar
- Helps reduce distractions and doom scrolling.

### 👁️ Content Visibility Controls
Choose exactly what appears in your YouTube experience:
- Show only videos from selected topics.
- Hide videos from unwanted categories.
- Hide "People also watched" recommendations.
- Hide trending or suggested sections.
- Hide community posts if desired.

### 📺 Channel Controls
- Whitelist favorite channels so their content is always visible.
- Blacklist channels you never want to see.
- Import and export channel lists.

### ⚙️ Personalized Feed
Create a YouTube experience tailored to your goals:
- Learning
- Career growth
- Education
- Business
- Productivity
- Technology

Instead of endless entertainment, your homepage becomes a curated learning dashboard.

### 🔄 Easy Toggle
- Enable or disable each filter independently.
- Turn the extension on or off with a single click.

### 💾 Save Preferences
All settings are stored locally so your filters remain active every time you open YouTube.

## Benefits
